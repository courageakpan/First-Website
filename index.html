Your Kitchen. Your Classroom

​​Relaxed Private Cooking Lessons with Chef Jeff Seligman*

  • Learn to cook delicious healthy meals in the privacy and safety of your own kitchen.
  • Learn to organize your kitchen and make the best use of limited space.
  • Learn to understand and appreciate fresh ingredients, good timing and knife skills.
  • Learn to cook for yourself, loved ones, friends, even host dinner parties.
  • Learn to hone and expand your culinary skills even if you're already an accomplished home cook.

*In-home private dining available. All photos were taken pre-pandemic. Chef Jeff now keeps a safe social distance at all times!
