In-Home Private Dining with Chef Jeff Seligman

Are you in the mood for a gourmet quality meal but still don’t feel safe going to a public restaurant? Invite Chef Jeff to your home to be your very own private chef. He’ll prepare a unique multi-course meal to your specifications—while keeping a safe social distance of course! He'll work with you to plan a delicious dining experience in the comfort of your own home, and create a special atmosphere for you and your friends and loved ones.

Chef Jeff Will Help You:

  • Plan a special meal that suits your tastes and the occasion, whether it's a romantic dinner, cocktail party, business lunch, you name it!
  • Shop for food items and ingredients that are fresh and in season to make a meal that is delicious and memorable.
  • Set up an ideal atmosphere for the meal, including making optimal use of meal preparation and serving space.
  • Prepare and enjoy a delicious and memorable multi-course meal in the privacy and safety of your own home.
